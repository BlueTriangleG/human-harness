Channel watchdog (`watchdog`): once a minute it looks at each channel of yours that runs through a bridge. A bridge that stops answering or stays disconnected is restarted and your owner is told; you get a line in the day's digest. A channel that will not come back by itself (signed out, credentials refused) it cannot mend: your owner has to see to it. When your main process does not answer or take its messages for three minutes it is restarted once; when your owner wrote several times and nothing of yours went out, the watchdog only tells your owner and leaves you alone. `watchdog status` shows what it sees and its last incidents. Your owner can also reach Claude Code directly with "/cc …": those messages are handled without you and never reach you.
