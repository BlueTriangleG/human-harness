# One project directory for one matter
Summary: For something that takes days, will have helpers on it and will see changes of mind, open a project directory: the proposal, the decisions, the material and the helpers' output all go in it, and reading it first thing the next day puts you back in.
When to open: When a big matter has just been decided on; when you want to find what was decided about something earlier.

## Practices
- The directory always holds: `README.md` (what is to be done, how far it is, the next step), `decisions.md` (what was decided, why, and on which day), and sub-directories for material and output.
- A helper sent on it works in this directory, and what it hands in goes here too.
- Before every stop, update "how far it is" and "the next step" in the README.

## Common pitfalls
- A decision is only said in chat, and days later nobody remembers why it was made.
- Material lies scattered, and next time the search starts over.
