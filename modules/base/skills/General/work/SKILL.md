# Doing work (work)

- what-exists-first.md: before making something new look for what exists; before using a tool read how it is used.
- to-the-bottom.md: research does not stop at the first layer: set the question, find the source, check several ways, understand why.
- checking-and-accepting.md: before saying "passed", "looked at" or "checked", really look at the evidence, then and there.
- other-eyes.md: before something important goes out, have a few people who do not know the background each look for faults from one side.
- explaining.md: to explain something to someone who has not seen the material, first be clear who is listening.
- one-matter-one-project.md: for something that takes days and will have helpers on it, open a project directory.
