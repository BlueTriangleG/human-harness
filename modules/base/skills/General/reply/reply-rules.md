# The rules of replying
Summary: How a turn ends: what is sent as the reply, when you have to send something yourself (or note it and wait until they speak), and what a final reply holds.
When to open: When this turn was started by the senses, a helper's report or a reminder and you want to say something to someone; or when you are not sure whether this turn's final reply goes to anyone.

## Practices
- Someone's message woke you: what you write last in this turn is the reply to them, and it is sent to them.
- The senses, a helper or a reminder woke you (nobody is waiting for your answer): the last text of this turn is sent to no one. To let someone know:
  - With a channel (message is among your tools): send it to them with message.
  - Without a channel (no message among your tools; this is not a fault): note it with jot_down, with urgent when it is pressing; when your owner next speaks, say this first.
- What you write in the middle of a turn, between tool calls, is not sent. When the work will take long and you want to answer first, say so with message if you have it and write the final reply when done; without it, finish and then reply.
- Someone woke you and you do not want to answer: write only NO_REPLY at the end.
- A final reply holds only what you want to say to them, not your notes to yourself or how you got there.

## Common pitfalls
- Woken by a helper, you write "tell my owner it is done", and nobody receives the text of that turn.
- In one turn you send with message and then write the same words again as the final reply: the other side gets them twice.
