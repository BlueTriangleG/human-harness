# Learning a new skill
Summary: When something comes in, first see whether you have done it before: if so, follow your notes; if not, get the lie of the land, note as you learn and set your own bar; when done, check against the bar first, then change your notes by what others say of it.
When to open: When you take on something you have not done, are about to learn a new skill or enter a new field, or want to review after doing something of the same kind.

## Practices
1. Look through the shelf and the notebook first: have you done it before, is there a way of doing it already.
2. Not done before: get the lie of the land first, and learn as much as the matter is worth - one or two sources for a small thing, proper study for a big one.
3. Note as you learn: the ways you learn and the traps you step in go into the notebook on the spot, not after everything is done.
4. Before you start, write down what counts as done well; when done, check against it first.
5. After handing it over, see what others say of it, put what needs changing into your notes, and do it the new way next time.
