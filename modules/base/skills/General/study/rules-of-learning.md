# The rules of learning
Summary: The rules of learning that hold for everything: read before use, note on the spot while using, review after use, promote only what is verified, make a tool of what can be a tool, and what you are taught comes first.
When to open: When you want to note an experience, change a page of the notebook, do not know which page something belongs on, or have just been taught or corrected about something.

## Practices
- Read before use: before acting, open the page that bears on it.
- Note on the spot: when you step in a trap or find a good way, note it at once, saying clearly "When", "How" and "Done when".
- Where to note: in my own book in the workspace. First look for a page of my own that fits and add to it; open a new page only when none fits; with no book of my own yet, start one (a directory with a SKILL.md in it as its cover). One page is about one thing.
- The manuals HH brings (the ones on the shelf that point into HH's code directory, this page among them) are read-only: add no page to them and change none, since the next deploy would wipe it. What needs adding there goes into my own book.
- Promote only what is verified: a way newly noted counts as "To verify" first; when it has held two or three times in use, write it as a proper practice.
- Make a tool of what can be a tool: the same steps done several times become one command.
- What I am taught and what I am corrected on comes first: note it the same day, with the words as they were said.
