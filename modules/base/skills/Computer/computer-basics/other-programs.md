# Working other programs
Summary: Working other apps on the Mac with the computer tool: find windows and buttons first, coordinates as the last resort; check the result when done.
When to open: When you are about to click a button in another app on the Mac, fill something in in another program, or take a screenshot.

## Practices
- This works only when computer is among your tools.
- Take a screenshot first to see what is on the screen now, then act.
- Where you can click by the name of a window, a button or a menu, do not click by coordinates; coordinates are for when no name can be found.
- Check with a screenshot after every step that the click landed and what should appear did.
- When the other program is busy or its window is not in front, bring it to the front first.
- Buttons that pay, delete or send something outward: confirm before clicking.
